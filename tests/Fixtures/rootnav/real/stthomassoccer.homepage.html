St. Thomas Soccer Club

The St Thomas Soccer Club is a non-profit organization, administered by volunteers who give generously of their time and talent to organize the beautiful game in St. Thomas. We serve the St. Thomas area, drawing players from the communities within Elgin County and it’s surrounding areas. Our regular season games, annual festivals and tournaments are played on pitches located throughout St. Thomas.  

We were established in 1955 with very few teams. Today, we have over 1500 players who register annually, comprising almost 160 teams. This includes youth recreational players from ages 4 to 18, as well as our recreational adult divisions.  Additionally, we have a growing competitive program, with over 20 competitive teams that travel and compete against various teams in various leagues within Ontario.
