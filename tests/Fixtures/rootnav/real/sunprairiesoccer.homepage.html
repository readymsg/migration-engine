Sun Prairie Soccer Club is a 501(c)(3) non-profit organization whose mission is to provide the youth of Sun Prairie, and surrounding communities, a high quality and affordable program to learn and play the game of soccer in an educational, challenging, safe, and inclusive environment. We offer year-round programming from 3-19 years old for players of all skill levels and experience, from recreational to competitive play. We are also the only accredited Youth Academy serving the east of side of Madison.

We focus on developing players at all levels, teaching technical and tactical skills, sportsmanship, teamwork, respect and fair play, while fostering an environment where players can enjoy the beautiful game of soccer.
